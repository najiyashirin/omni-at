Meet our founder

Built by an educator, not a corporation.

One professor’s belief that every learner deserves to be heard.

Omni-at English was founded by a dedicated professor with a simple idea: English learning should be personal, encouraging and connected to the way each student actually thinks.

After years of teaching, mentoring and observing where traditional classrooms leave learners behind, our founder created a more human approach—one that combines academic expertise with patient guidance, meaningful conversation and measurable progress.

“We do more than teach English. We help learners find the confidence to express who they are.”
Professor & Founder Omni-at English
